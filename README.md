## About Me

I am **Hifajul Islam**, an **M.Sc. Student in Agriculture (Computer Application)** at the **ICAR–Indian Agricultural Statistics Research Institute (ICAR-IASRI), New Delhi, India**. My work sits at the intersection of agricultural science and artificial intelligence, focusing on reliable deep learning and computer vision systems for real-world crop health analysis.

## Featured Research Projects

### 1. Brinjal Disease and Insect Pest Classification
![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-In%20Progress-059669?style=flat-square) ![Topic](https://img.shields.io/badge/Architecture-ResNet50-0A192F?style=flat-square) ![Domain](https://img.shields.io/badge/Domain-Deep%20Learning-0D1117?style=flat-square)

- **Objective:** Automated image-based classification of brinjal crop diseases and insect pests to support timely agricultural diagnosis.
- **Approach:** Applying Convolutional Neural Networks and **ResNet50** transfer learning for multi-class visual feature extraction.

### 2. Open Set Recognition for Image Classification
![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-In%20Progress-059669?style=flat-square) ![Topic](https://img.shields.io/badge/Method-OpenMax-0A192F?style=flat-square) ![Domain](https://img.shields.io/badge/Domain-Open%20Set%20Recognition-0D1117?style=flat-square)

- **Objective:** Enable image classifiers to recognize known classes accurately while flagging unfamiliar or unseen categories rather than misclassifying them.
- **Approach:** Investigating **OpenMax** and related open set recognition techniques for reliable real-world deployment.

### 3. Cotton Disease and Insect Pest Image Classification
![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-In%20Progress-059669?style=flat-square) ![Topic](https://img.shields.io/badge/Domain-Computer%20Vision-0A192F?style=flat-square) ![Stack](https://img.shields.io/badge/Models-CNN-0D1117?style=flat-square)

- **Objective:** Build visual recognition pipelines to identify diseases and insect pests affecting cotton crops.
- **Approach:** Exploring deep learning and image preprocessing workflows for agricultural image classification.
